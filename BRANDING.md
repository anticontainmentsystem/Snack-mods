# Snack brand assets

Snack is the app. Snacks are its windows. Condiments are separately installed additions.

- [Square Snack icon](assets/snack-icon.svg): scalable favicon and square app/avatar artwork, white background.
- [Snack logo emoji](assets/snack-emoji.svg): transparent custom emoji, square canvas. This is custom artwork, not a Unicode character.
- [Condiments logo](assets/snack-condiments.svg): the Snack slab dressed in gently animated sauce. It respects reduced-motion preferences.
- [Party table](assets/party-snack-table.svg): original pixel artwork used inside the Wallpaper preview Snacks.

Simple Agent Chat includes its own icon and emoji assets. Choose **Snack** in its emoji picker to insert `:snack:` into a message or use it as a reaction. That token is rendered as logo artwork in this Condiment. Other clients may display the readable token.

Third-party Condiments own their branding. These assets are optional examples; Snack does not require authors to use its icons, title, emoji or artwork. Bundle everything your own interface needs alongside your Condiment.
